# Worked example: Kadri Coffee

A small brand layer made with Poligraf from a minimal hand-in: the form the owner filled in and the encoded brand. It holds what you need for the exercises and the cold handoff, not the whole run.

| File | What it is | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| `hand-in/brand-folder.md` | The filled form: what the owner handed in (answers 1 to 7) | Exercise 1 |
| `brand/BRAND.md` | The encoded brand, in words: every value, its status, the fonts, the voice, the files, and the traps (its Refuse section) | Exercises 1, 3, 4 and 5 |
| `brand/tokens.css` | The brand tokens and the per-scheme rules | Exercises 1, 2 and 5 |
| `brand/locked.json` | The status of every value: you said (`locked`) or we guessed (`derived`) | Exercise 5 |
| `brand/fonts.css` and `brand/fonts/` | Hubot Sans (display) and Mona Sans (body). Part of the brand, free under OFL 1.1, licences beside them | Exercise 5 |
| `brand/assets/logo.svg` | The coral k | Exercises 2 and 5 |
| `brand/assets/logo-reversed.svg` | The dark k, used on the coral surface | Exercises 2 and 5 |

For the cold handoff (`workshop/cold-handoff.md`), attach `examples/kadri/brand/`.

The first sheet's break (the coral k vanishes on the coral surface, `logo-visible`) and its fix (the dark k, chosen in `tokens.css`; the layout did not change) are in `showcase.html` at the root of Brand Asset Maker (BAM), with the story and a Do and a Don't for each layout. Exercise 2 uses them.

The full worked run stays with the facilitator: the intake log, the voice file, the full list of traps, the feedback notes, the decisions, the generators and every post. When `BRAND.md` names `feedback/0001`, `feedback/0002`, `voice.md`, `intake.log.md`, `brand.json`, `fonts.md`, a generator or a proposal, it points to that run. `BRAND.md` copies their values, so the folder stands on its own.

What the intake guessed beside what Madhuri's brand guide says (the answer key) is revealed in session one.
