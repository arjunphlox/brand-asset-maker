# Voice

**Tone words** (supplied, locked; hand-in question 5, verbatim: "Direct, warm, concrete."): direct · warm · concrete.

**Approved lines:** none supplied. Questions 5 to 7 gave tone words, a never-say and two photographs, not lines to reuse. The two posts show copy printed on the bag ("COFFEE FOR YOUR EVERYDAY.", "GOOD COFFEE. YOUR WAY.", "WHOLE BEAN COFFEE", "250 g", tasting notes). That copy was read off a photograph, not handed in as copy, so it is **derived** here: proposals may use it verbatim, and it becomes an approved line only when the owner says so.

**Never claims:**
- Never imply certifications, health benefits or a sourcing story we cannot prove (supplied, locked; hand-in question 6, verbatim: "Never imply certifications, health benefits or a sourcing story we cannot prove."). This covers "organic", "fair trade", "ethically sourced", "single origin", "direct trade", "farm to cup", "boosts focus", "good for you", "antioxidants", and any named farm, region or producer.
- No invented numbers (derived): the only number in the hand-in is "250 g", printed on the bag in both posts. No customer counts, cups sold, ratings or percentages until the owner supplies a sourced figure.
- No invented quotes from people (derived, from question 6's "cannot prove"): no customer, barista or reviewer quotes unless the owner supplies the person and the words. The brand may quote itself.

**Style** (derived): short declaratives; name the flavour ("peach, citrus and honey"), not the feeling; "you" and "your", not "consumers"; no exclamation marks. The bag sets its lines in capitals with a full stop; posts may copy them that way.
