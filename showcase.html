Poligraf, on one page

Your brand file, three layouts, and checks that measure every post. The posts below are from the practice brand, Kadri Coffee. Nothing on them was edited by hand: every fix went into a file, and the post was made again.

Kadri Coffee is fictional teaching material by Madhuri Maram, used with permission.

How a sheet speaks

Every post on a contact sheet gets one of three labels, with the check names beside it in small type.

  • must fix


    A check failed. Name the break and the one file it belongs in.

  • your call


    Every check passed, but a note asks for a brand decision, such as a logo that shows only faintly.

  • fine


    Every check passed. Now ask the two-second question.

The two-second question: what do you read first? It should be the first slot of the layout (the headline, the number, the quote). If it is not, it is not a pick, even when every check passes.

Before and after: one break, one file

Kadri's first sheet failed on the coral surface: the coral k vanished. The break was the logo (kind locked). The fix went into the brand file: the owner's dark k is used on coral. The layout did not change.

A story is a new job, not a bigger picture

A story (9:16) is a reminder: one fact, and what to do. The layout adapts: the number moves to the centre line, clear of the app's header and reply bar. A feed post is never stretched into a story.

What each check means

Each check has a name (small type) and a plain line. The sheet shows the plain line.
